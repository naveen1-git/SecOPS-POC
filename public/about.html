Our Mission

Our mission is to help individuals stay organized, focused, and productive throughout their day. Whether you are managing daily personal chores or tracking work projects, our task manager offers a clean and intuitive interface to manage your work effortlessly.

Key Features

  • Create, edit, and delete tasks instantly
  • Mark tasks as completed with a single click
  • Persistent SQLite storage for reliability
  • Clean, responsive user interface
